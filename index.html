Where the numbers happen: the in-app journey, measured

The metrics course told you WHAT an online store's numbers are. This one tells you WHERE they happen - on which screens, along which paths, after which taps. A leader track that reads the journey without computing: the screen map and its five entry doors, why search users convert multiples of browsers, what actually makes a product page sell (reviews math included), what kills checkouts (Baymard's honest reasons list), and how to prioritize journey fixes. And an analyst track that measures it all on Mango Lane - the same fashion app, the same month, every count reconciling to the metrics course to the order. The centerpiece is a live flow simulator: toggle four classic journey fixes (smarter search, richer PDP, persistent cart, one-page checkout) and watch conversion climb from 2.7% to 4.0% and GMV rise 47% - every number recomputed live. No install, no account, no bill.
